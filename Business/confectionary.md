# PESTLE
# Political
- Governments require food producers to clearly label nutritional information.
- There are regulations on sugar levels and warnings related to health.
- Companies must follow strict labelling laws about ingredients and sourcing.
- New policies aimed at improving public health put pressure on confectionary companies.
- Businesses may face fines or penalties if they do not comply with food regulations.

# Economic
- Changes in consumer income levels affect demand for treats and luxury sweets.
- During economic downturns, shopers may switch to cheaper brands or buy fewer cofectionary products.
- Rising costs of raw ingredients increase production costs.
- Global supply chain issues can make ingredient prices unstable.

# Social
- Growing public concern about health, obesity and sugar consumption recuces demand for sugary sweets.
- Increasing interest in healthier alternatives such as low sugar, vegan and gluten-free or organic confectionary.
- Lifestyle trends are shifting consumer behaviour away from high-sugar snacks.
- Parents are buying fewer sweets due to worries about children's sugar intake.
- Social media trends influences which brands, flavours or novelty sweets become popular.

# Technology
- Use of Technology in production.
- Automation and advanced Technology.
- E-commerce and online sales.
- Digital Marketing and Social Media.

# Legal factors
- Confectionary businesses must follow legal requirements for food labelling, including ingredients, allergens and nutritional
  information.
- Labelling and sugar-content regulations increase operating costs for confectionary producers.
- Nathasha's Law (UK, 2021) requires clear, full ingredient and allergen lavels on all Pre-packed for Direct Sale (PPDS) foods.
- Employment laws change regularly, so businesses must ensure compliance to avoid legal penalties

# Environmental 
- Chocolate manufacturers are heavily affected by the price volatility of raw matterials, especially cocoa. 
- Climate change and poor weather conditions in cocoa producing regions cause reduced harvests and higher cocoa prices.
- Rising costs of raw ingredients force confectionary businesses to increase product prices.
- Between 24-25, food pruces rose significantly, with chocolate among the most impacted categories.
